Supplementary Fig. 20. Motor feature decoder optimization and example channel recording.
(a) Example representation of a band-pass filtered (0.3 – 300 Hz) channel recording (ii) and
spectrogram (iii) aligned to the subject’s wrist velocity in y-direction (front-back) (i). (b) Example
representation of a channel power spectral density (PSD) before and after removing
hemodynamics (HD). Dashed lines indicate the band-pass filter range (3 – 12 Hz) that was applied
to extract the HD time series components. (c) Linear parametric sweep of the number of partial
least squares (PLS) components, and the resulting predictive error sum of squares (PRESS) (top)
and Pearson’s correlation coefficient (bottom) between observed motor feature (normalized wrist
velocity in y-direction) and PLS prediction. Data are presented as mean ± SE (n=5 cross-validation
folds; 3,420 samples per fold). Plots in (a-ii), (a-iii) and (b) after HD removal represent the same
data.
