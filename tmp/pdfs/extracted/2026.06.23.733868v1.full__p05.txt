              Benchtop characterization in phosphate-buffered saline using broadband chirp excitation
    confirmed a uniform frequency response from DC to 250Hz with minimal channel-to-channel

    stochastic spread of bias currents (the range varied based on bias voltages), pointing to the need
    for future pixel architectures to address           compensation of device process variation (i.e. threshold

    measured total input-referred RMS noise of ~60µVrms. These benchtop validations established
    the electrical functionality and uniformity of the platform prior to                 in vivo recordings.
