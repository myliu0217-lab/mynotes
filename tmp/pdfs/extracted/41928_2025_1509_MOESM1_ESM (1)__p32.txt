Supplementary Fig. 4. Titanium nitride characterization. (a) The characteristic roughened
morphology of the deposited TiN films was observed by SEM (FEI Helios dual-beam FIB-SEM).
(b) Surface roughness of the TiN was quantified using AFM (Bruker Dimension Icon, tapping
mode) yielding a root mean square roughness of 3.9 nm. The TiN cyclic voltammetry (CV) was
measured using a 1 cm2 TiN working electrode, Ag/AgCl pellet reference electrode, and Pt coil
counter electrode in 1× PBS between -1.3 and 1.3 V at a scan rate of 100 mV/s. TiN was also
characterized using Raman spectroscopy (Renishaw inVia) and XPS elemental analysis (PHI
VersaProbe II) with peak fitting (PHI MultiPak). (c) Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (CH
Instruments 760D) was performed to verify the reduction in electrode impedance of roughened
TiN compared to smooth surface electrodes. A 20 mV sinusoidal voltage ranging from 106 to 10-1
Hz was applied to characterize a 20 µm × 20 µm working TiN test electrode (red) in 10× PBS with
a large area Pt coil counter electrode and Ag/AgCl reference electrode. A 25 µm × 25 µm Au
electrode (blue) measured in 1x PBS is shown for comparison. Equivalent circuit model fitting,
consisting of a double layer capacitor (constant phase element CPE with leakage resistance Rct) at
the electrode surface, solution resistance (Rs), and parasitic capacitance (Cp) was performed in
ZView software (Scribner Associates).
