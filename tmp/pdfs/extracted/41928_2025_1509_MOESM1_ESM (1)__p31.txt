Supplementary Fig. 3. Coupon processing. A cross-section through the microelectrode array
(MEA) region shows the pristine wafer with bulk Si substrate (i) post-processed to expose the
electrodes (ii and iii) followed by lift-off patterning of a protective TiN layer (iv), spin-on

RST (remaining silicon thickness) (vii), parylene encapsulation (viii), and solvent release (ix).
